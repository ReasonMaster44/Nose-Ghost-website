About

Born out of a drunken night out in Camden, Nose Ghost live and breathe the rock-n-roll dream. The four-piece indie outfit hailing from London have been moving from strength to strength since they first hit the stage in 2024.

Their debut album, “The Debrief” has nothing brief about it. It’s been cooking up at their home studio in North London for almost 2 years under Asher Saipe’s tight production. The guitars soar, the vocals growl, the drums punch with stunning precision, everything has been crafted to fit the most into this 45 minute debut. Punk. Surf. Indie. Nose Ghost do it all.

Expect pop masterpieces like the single “South”, fast and loose riffs on “Jamie Clarke” and “Mr Rent”, and singalong anthems like the single “Anaconda Malt Liquor” and a newly re-recorded version of “Bubbles”, first released in July 2024.
